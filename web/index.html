undertone

Hide your voice inside a song.

Speakers play the song. Earphones play the song and you.

  1. Record your message

    Speak softly in your normal voice, close to the mic. Short and slow works best.

  2. Choose the song

    Full, continuous sound hides your voice best. Any audio or video works.

  3. Place your voice

    Drag your voice onto a full part of the song. The line under it shows how well it stays hidden on phone speakers.

    hidden might be faintly heard up close song too quiet here

  4. Make it

    On earphones the song plays on the left and your voice on the right. On a speaker it's just the song.

    Songs with gaps can let a faint voice through on phones with two speakers. Rain under the song, or a little song in the right ear, covers those gaps.

Everything runs in your browser. Your recording and song are never uploaded.
